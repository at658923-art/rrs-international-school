08 YOUR INFORMATION

Clear and simple

Privacy & terms.

These notes explain what this enquiry form does with the information you choose to provide.

Terms of enquiry

Submitting the form asks R.R.S International School to contact you about your enquiry. It does not confirm admission, a place, or any school service. Please provide accurate contact details and avoid sharing sensitive documents in the message.

You can contact the school directly at +91 91234 70121 if you need to update or discuss an enquiry.

Privacy policy

The form collects the student name, parent or guardian mobile number, email address, class of interest, and your message. On submission, these details are sent to the school’s configured Google Apps Script and spreadsheet so the school can review and respond.

This page cannot verify spreadsheet access, retention, or deletion. Contact the school to ask about an enquiry record or request an update.

Cookies & data

The site uses one first-party cookie only to remember your cookie-preference choice. It does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Google Maps and Pexels video previews are external services governed by their own privacy practices.

The website does not store enquiry details in cookies or browser storage. Details are sent only when you select Send enquiry. The video previews load only after you press Play.
